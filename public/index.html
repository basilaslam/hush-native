Entirely native.

This deployment coordinates rooms for the Hush Rust desktop app. It does not contain a browser voice client.

Open Hush on your computer and enter this deployment’s HTTPS origin in Voice & settings.

Native Opus audio travels directly between authenticated peers over QUIC. This Vercel service exchanges room membership and connection details only.

Protocol: hush-voice/1 · API: POST /api/native-room
A private Vercel Blob store must be attached for production room coordination.
